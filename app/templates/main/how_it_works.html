Your Investment Journey, Step by Step

STEP 1

Register & Verify Your Identity

Create your account using your email address. After registration, complete our KYC (Know Your Customer) verification by submitting a government-issued ID, selfie, and proof of address. Your account will be fully verified within 24-48 hours.

Why KYC? It protects you and the platform from fraud and ensures regulatory compliance.

Choose Your Investment Plan

Browse our available investment plans ranging from Starter to Elite. Each plan has a defined ROI percentage, duration, minimum deposit, and profit distribution method (daily or at maturity). Choose the plan that fits your financial goals.

STEP 3

Fund Your Account

Select your preferred cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB). The system provides you with our secure wallet address. Transfer the required amount and upload your payment proof (screenshot). Your deposit will be reviewed and approved by our admin team, typically within 24 hours.

STEP 4

Profits Are Generated Automatically

Once your deposit is approved and your investment plan is activated, the system automatically calculates and credits profits to your account according to your plan schedule. Daily profit plans credit every 24 hours. Fixed maturity plans credit the full ROI at plan completion.

Our investment team manages all portfolios. You do not need to actively trade or monitor markets.
STEP 5

Withdraw Your Earnings

Submit a withdrawal request from your dashboard at any time (subject to your plan's withdrawal conditions). Enter your wallet address, amount, and your secure withdrawal PIN. Requests are reviewed and processed within 24-48 hours. You will receive email notifications at every stage.

Security PIN: Withdrawal PIN is set during account setup. Never share your PIN with anyone, including our support team.

How Profits Are Calculated & Distributed

DAILY

Daily Profit Plans

Profits are calculated daily based on your invested amount and ROI %. Yield is credited automatically to your available balance every 24 hours, giving you liquidity and compounding options.

Invested: $1,000 | ROI: 10% monthly | Daily Profit: ~$3.33/day
AT MATURITY

Fixed Maturity Plans

Your full returns are locked for the duration of the plan. Accumulating ROI yield is credited as a lump sum immediately when the plan duration expires, optimizing larger quant block moves.

Invested: $5,000 | ROI: 15% | Duration: 60 days | Payout at end: $750

**Capital Return Policy**: Depending on the strategy configured by the administration team, your principal investment is returned safely to your available balance at plan completion, or built directly into active compounding swaps.
