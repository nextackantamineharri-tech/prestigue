Investment Risk Warning

Cryptocurrency investments involve significant risk of loss. The value of digital assets can fluctuate dramatically. Past performance displayed on this platform does not guarantee future results. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

Types of Risk You Should Understand

Market Volatility Risk

Cryptocurrency prices are highly volatile and can change significantly within short periods. The ROI projections in our investment plans are based on plan parameters, not guaranteed market performance.

Platform Risk

Like all digital platforms, Prestige Finance Global is subject to technical failures, cyberattacks, and operational disruptions. While we maintain robust security, no system is entirely risk-free.

Regulatory Risk

The regulatory environment for cryptocurrency is evolving globally. Changes in laws or government actions could affect the platform's operations in certain jurisdictions.

Liquidity Risk

Withdrawal processing is subject to admin review and may take 24-48 hours. In extreme circumstances, withdrawals may be delayed further. Investment plan conditions may restrict early withdrawals.

Currency Risk

Deposits are made in cryptocurrency. The value of your deposited asset in fiat currency terms may change between the time of deposit and the time of withdrawal, independent of any platform-generated returns.

Counterparty Risk

You are trusting Prestige Finance Global to manage and return your assets. While we operate with full transparency and admin oversight, you should only invest amounts you are comfortable committing to this arrangement.
